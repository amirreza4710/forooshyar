# ADR-012 — Distribution Core Completeness (A2.5) & Phase A3 Domain Admission

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Component:** Domain Model / Sales Core / Cash & Delivery Operations  
**References:** `ADR-0001`, `ADR-010`, `ADR-011`, `docs/00_Project/Roadmap.md`

---

## Context

Phase A1 is frozen and Phase A2 core entities (`visits`, `targets`, `creditLimit` link on `orders`) are verified. Product owner review of the real-world Iranian capillary distribution loop (`پخش مویرگی`) identified operational completeness gaps that block real-world pilot deployment:

1. Missing product cost/packaging/threshold metadata (`costPrice`, `unitsPerPack`, `minStock`) preventing gross profit and low-stock visibility.
2. Missing customer zone/location fields (`zone`, `latitude`, `longitude`) and optional rep zone assignment (`assignedZone`) needed for capillary routing and rep data isolation.
3. Missing order discount, payment method, bonus/free-goods row support (`isBonus`), and atomic stock restoration on order cancellation/soft-delete.
4. Phase A3 operational execution domains committed in `Roadmap.md`: Cash & Cheque Collections (`COL-F01..F03`), Delivery Dispatch & Partial Returns (`DEL-F01..F02`), Traceable Sales Commissions (`COM-F01..F03`), and Executive Morning Alerts (`ALT-F01..F03`).

## Decision

1. **Wave 1 (`Phase A2.5 — Distribution Integrity`):**
   - Extend `productsTable` with additive defaulted columns: `costPrice` (default `0`), `unitsPerPack` (default `1`), `minStock` (default `10`).
   - Extend `customersTable` with optional `zone`, `latitude`, `longitude`.
   - Extend `usersTable` with optional `assignedZone`.
   - Extend `ordersTable` with `subtotal`, `discount`, `paymentMethod` (`CASH | CARD | CREDIT | CHEQUE`), and `notes`; allow server-enforced `isBonus` order items (`price = 0`, stock decremented) and server-side total calculation `Math.max(0, subtotal - discount)`.
   - Enforce atomic inventory restoration (`stock + qty` under `FOR UPDATE` row lock) whenever an active non-cancelled order transitions to `"لغو شده"` or is soft-deleted.
   - Enforce rep-level data isolation on `GET /orders` and `GET /visits`, and optional zone isolation on `GET /customers`.

2. **Wave 2 (`Phase A3 — Cash, Delivery, Commissions & Alerts`):**
   - Add `collectionsTable` (`CASH`, `CARD`, `CHEQUE` with `chequeNumber`, `sayyadCode`, `dueDate`, `status`: `PENDING | CLEARED | BOUNCED`) with transactional updates to `customers.balance` and bounced-cheque blocking on new orders.
   - Add `deliveriesTable` (`ASSIGNED | OUT_FOR_DELIVERY | DELIVERED | PARTIAL_RETURN | FAILED`) supporting loading-sheet aggregation and partial return settlement (restoring returned stock and reducing order total/customer balance atomically).
   - Provide traceable `/api/commissions/summary` (derived from verified non-cancelled orders, cleared collections, and targets; zero hard-coded mock numbers) and `/api/alerts/summary` (Low Stock, Dormant Customers > 14d, Rep Inactivity, Overdue/Bounced Cheques & Near Credit Limit).

3. **Stack & Boundary Preservation:**
   - Continues strictly within PostgreSQL + Drizzle ORM + Express 5 + OpenAPI + React 19 (`ADR-0001`), preserving modular monolith boundaries and transaction-level audit logging.

## Consequences

- `lib/db/src/schema/*` and `lib/api-spec/openapi.yaml` are extended additively with safe defaults so existing Phase A1/A2 integration and E2E suites remain green.
- All Wave 1 & Wave 2 backend endpoints expose generated Zod validators and React Query hooks via `@workspace/api-zod` and `@workspace/api-client-react` for consumption by the frontend.
