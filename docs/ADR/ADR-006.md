# ADR-006: Pricing & Revenue Architecture

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Farakhorasan is preparing a 30-day operational pilot and has not validated Product-Market Fit. Commercial architecture must remain flexible, auditable, and economically sustainable without creating a premature Billing Engine.

## Decision

- Adopt a Hybrid SaaS Revenue Model.
- Treat all current price figures and discounts as Pricing Hypotheses rather than an official Price List.
- Offer a Paid Design Partner Pilot before publishing standard SaaS plans.
- Use Base Organization Subscription, Billable Seat allowances and bundles, optional add-ons, Metered Services, one-time implementation revenue, and Enterprise Support as the commercial building blocks.
- Define Billable Seat independently from RBAC and organizational roles.
- Determine billability through configurable, versioned, auditable Business Activity rules.
- Treat AI, API, SMS, WhatsApp, calls, email, and similar variable-cost services as Metered Services.
- Keep prices, discounts, periods, allowances, and contract versions configurable and versioned.
- Keep Billing Engine, Subscription Management, and automated invoicing outside the 30-day MVP.
- Use manual invoicing and an auditable usage export during the pilot.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Avoids premature public pricing commitments.
- Preserves future multi-tenant and partner-aware pricing options.
- Aligns monetization with measurable customer value.
- Prevents RBAC and job-title changes from breaking billing.
- Makes variable service costs visible.

### Trade-offs

- Pilot billing is manual.
- Pricing validation depends on evidence from paid pilots.
- Public plan packaging is deferred.
- Future automation requires a versioned price book, entitlement model, and usage ledger.

## Guardrails

- Core Operations identity must not be weakened by moving collection, KPI, commission, or foundational rules into arbitrary add-ons.
- No hard-coded production price is allowed.
- No Billing Engine implementation is authorized by this ADR.
