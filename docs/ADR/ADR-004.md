# ADR-004: AI acts only as an assistant

## Status
Proposed

## Context
AI can improve productivity and recommendations, but final business decisions require deterministic controls and human accountability.

## Decision
AI will only suggest, summarize, classify, or assist. It must not be the final authority for business-critical decisions.

## Consequences
- Human or deterministic system approval remains required for critical actions.
- AI outputs must be explainable enough to review.
