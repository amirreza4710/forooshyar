# ADR-007: Partner Commercial Model

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Farakhorasan may later sell and implement through referral partners, resellers, implementation partners, and technology partners. Modeling these as a linear maturity ladder would prevent a single partner from holding multiple capabilities and would blur revenue attribution with channel cost.

## Decision

- Model Referral, Resale, Implementation, and Technology Integration as independent Partner Capabilities.
- Allow one Partner to hold multiple capabilities under a versioned Partner Agreement.
- Treat partner-sourced revenue as revenue attribution, not a separate revenue source.
- Treat commissions and payouts as Channel Cost.
- Reserve the following concepts for future commercial architecture:
  - Agreement Version
  - Customer Attribution
  - Attribution Window
  - Commission Rule
  - Renewal Eligibility
  - Clawback
  - Payout Ledger
  - Delegated Tenant Administration
  - Partner Access Audit Log
- Require customer authorization for delegated administration.
- Keep Partner Portal and automated payouts outside the MVP.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Supports several channel strategies without redesigning tenant billing.
- Separates commercial attribution, commission calculation, and product revenue.
- Preserves auditability and contract versioning.

### Trade-offs

- Pilot partner operations remain manual.
- Future partner tooling requires explicit authorization and payout controls.
- Partner economics must be validated before implementation.
